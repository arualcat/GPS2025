Submission Topics

We invite original papers and presentations on the following topics (but not limited to):

  • Positioned in the Seven Mountains of Influence (Choose one):
    • Government

    • Business and Finance
    • Arts and Culture
    • Media
    • Religion
  • Positioned in Mental Health
  • Marketplace Positioning

Interdisciplinary and innovative approaches are highly encouraged.

How to Submit

Please prepare an abstract (max 300 words) and a short bio (max 100 words).
Submit your proposal via email to globalpositioningsymposium@gmail.com with the subject line "GPS 2025 Submission".

Include your name, affiliation, and preferred topic area.
Accepted presenters will be notified by email.

About the Symposium

The Global Positioning Symposium brings together thought leaders, practitioners, and scholars to explore how individuals and organizations can be strategically positioned for impact in today's world. Join us for two days of inspiration, learning, and networking.

For questions or more information, contact us at globalpositioningsymposium@gmail.com
